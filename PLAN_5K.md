| Deliverable | Hours |

| E-com store | 8–10 |

Over 29 days that's **~1.7 hrs/day delivering**, leaving 2.5–4 hrs/day for outreach. It fits — but only if you protect the outreach block. The classic failure is landing two clients in week 2, disappearing into delivery, and closing nothing in week 3.

**Rule: outreach happens before delivery, every single day.** Even at 30 minutes.
