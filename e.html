    Overview

    The better way to job search. Quick apply and autofill job applications in one click. Track jobs, companies, resumes & more!

    The Autofill Tool and Career Copilot for Job Applications – used by 1,000,000+ job seekers. Simplify Copilot is an AI tool designed to help you job search more efficiently. Autofill job applications, generate tailored resumes and cover letters, and track every step of your job search–all in one place. ✦ Built by Job Seekers, for Job Seekers Ever spent dozens of hours filling out the same application details over and over? We’ve been there too. That’s why we built Simplify Copilot: a smart, efficient way to job search that actually works and has helped applicants submit over 100 million applications and land thousands of job offers. ⭐ Our Features ⭐ ✔️ Autofill Applications on All Major ATSs Automate job applications across thousands of job boards and ATS platforms like Workday, Lever, Greenhouse, and more—no limits, free forever. ✔️ Identify Missing Keywords in Your Resume We analyze the job description you're applying to and shows you the most important keywords missing from your resume. ✔️ AI-Powered Resumes & Cover Letters Generate tailored, ATS-friendly resumes and personalized cover letters using our recruiter-approved AI. Our free resume builder has been used to create over 1,000,000 resumes. ✔️ Personalized Job Recommendations Tell us your dealbreakers (minimum salary, location, etc.) and we'll match you with jobs that fit YOU. No more endlessly scrolling on job boards. ✔️ All-In-One Job Tracker Bookmark jobs anywhere on the web and track your submitted applications, LinkedIn profiles, referrals, and interview notes from one simple dashboard. 🔍 Why Job Seekers Love Simplify - Save Hours on Applications: Cut application time from 10+ minutes to seconds - Apply to More Jobs: Users report applying to 3-5x more positions with the same effort - Higher Interview Rate: Our AI resume builder helps get your resume past ATS filters - Stay Organized: No more forgotten applications or missed follow-ups with our job tracker - Reduce Job Search Stress: Automate the most frustrating and repetitive parts of job hunting - Works Almost Everywhere: Compatible with millions of job boards and company career sites 🔐 Our Stance on Privacy We're a <10-person applied AI research lab based in San Francisco and take your privacy very seriously. While we do require permission to access pages for autofill functionality, we do NOT sell your data to advertisers. We are committed to protecting your data – you can read more about our take on privacy at https://simplify.jobs/about ✦ The better way to job search. Free to use with premium features available. By downloading this extension, you agree to our Terms of Service (https://simplify.jobs/terms) and Privacy Policy (https://simplify.jobs/privacy). Need help? Reach out anytime at support@simplify.jobs
