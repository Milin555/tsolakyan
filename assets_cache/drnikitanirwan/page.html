Dr Nikita Nirwan BDSAbout

Cosmetic Dentist & Facial Aesthetics

Qualified BDS King's College London (KCL) 2014

Dr Nirwan holds advanced qualifications in Botulinum toxin, dermal fillers, skin boosters and other non- invasive skin rejuvenation procedures. She has attended numerous postgraduate courses, masterclasses and regularly attends facial aesthetics conferences to keep up to date with the latest techniques and products. Her ethos is to deliver natural looking and undetectable results for her clients, keeping in mind their goals.

Dr Nirwan has also undertaken many dental postgraduate courses, taking a special interest in cosmetic orthodontics. She is an approved, certified provider of Invisalign, Fastbraces and Inman aligner.

She devotes a tremendous amount of time to her continued lifelong learning by attending courses, hands-on learning and conferences. She will also be undertaking a diploma in Aesthetic and Restorative Dentistry from the University of Warwick this year.

Dr Nirwan takes pride in delivering high quality, ethical treatment to her patients; with a passion for exceeding their expectations with her artistic flair and excellent clinical skills.
