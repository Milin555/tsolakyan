## WHERE TO GO

**Best targets, in order:**

| Business | Why |
|---|---|
| **Dental clinics** | Your demos are literally dental. Instant proof. |
| **Salons / spas** | Visual business, no website, owner on site |
| **Gyms / fitness** | Need class schedules + enquiries |
| **Real estate offices** | High deal value, they understand marketing |
| **Coaching / tuition centres** | Need credibility online |
| **Cafés / restaurants** | Lower budget — leave for later |

**Pick ONE commercial street. Do 10 in a row.** Don't travel between areas — walking time kills volume.

**Check first (30 seconds):** Google the business name on your phone. **No website = your best prospect.** Bad website = second best.

---

## THE OPENING (memorise this)

> "Hi — I build websites for businesses here. I made one for a clinic like yours. Can I show you? 30 seconds."

Then **hand them your phone.** Don't describe it. Let them scroll.

**Why handing the phone works:** they physically hold it, they scroll, they engage. It stops being a pitch and becomes a thing they're looking at.

---

## AFTER THEY LOOK

> "I can make one exactly like this for your business. Live in 2 days. ₹25,000."

**Then stop talking.** Say nothing. Let them speak first.

The silence is uncomfortable. Hold it anyway. Whoever speaks first loses the negotiation.

---

## WHAT THEY'LL SAY

**"How much again?"**
> ₹25,000 for the full 6-page site. ₹15,000 if you want the smaller 3-page version.

**"Too expensive"**
> The 3-page version is ₹15,000 and covers the basics — home, services, contact. Want that?

*Reduce pages, never the price.*

**"We already have a website"**
> Can I see it? *(look at it on your phone)* … It works, but on mobile customers have to scroll a lot before they find your number. Mine puts it at the top. Want me to make one and show you? Free — you only pay if you like it.

**"Send me details on WhatsApp"**
> Sure — what's your number? *(save it immediately)* I'll send the demo tonight. Can I call you Thursday for your answer?

*Always leave with a number AND a next step.*

**"I'll think about it"**
> No problem. What do you want to think about — the price, or whether you need a website?

*This gets the real objection out.*

**"Owner isn't here"**
> When does he/she come in? *(note it)* I'll come back then. Can I leave my number?

**"Who are you? Which company?"**
> I'm independent — that's why it's ₹25,000 and 2 days instead of ₹1 lakh and 2 months.

*Never claim to be a company. It falls apart on the second question.*

**"Do you have other clients here?"**
> These are sites I've built — *(show portfolio)*. Judge the work directly. I'm also happy to build yours first and you pay only if you like it.

*Never invent clients. Once you have real ones, name them.*

---

## THE CLOSE

> "I'll take ₹12,500 now and ₹12,500 when it's live. Scan this."

*(open UPI QR)*

**Get half up front. Always.** Someone who won't pay half won't pay in full later.

**If they hesitate on paying today:**
> I'll build it first and show you. If you like it you pay, if not you don't. Just give me your logo and a few photos.

*This closes the nervous ones. Your delivery cost is 4 hours — worth the risk.*

---

## WHAT TO DO WHEN IT'S GOING BADLY

**Nobody will talk to you** → wrong time. Avoid lunch hour and closing time. Best window: 11am–1pm and 4–6pm.

**They look but don't buy** → you're pitching too long. Show, price, stop talking.

**"Send WhatsApp" every time** → you're not asking for the close. Ask directly: *"Want me to build it?"*

**Owner never present** → ask the staff when they come, and come back then. Staff cannot buy.

**5 days, zero sales** → drop to ₹12,000 express tier and rebuild from there.

---

## THE ONLY RULE

**10 doors a day.** Everything else is detail.

You will get rejected 8 or 9 times out of 10. That's the normal shape of this — not a signal that it's failing.
