## 6. Scripts

### 6.1 UK cold call — front desk gatekeeper

> "Hi, this is Milin — I'm not selling anything over the phone, I just need thirty seconds. I redesigned your practice's website and put it online so Dr. [Name] could look at it. Is he/she around, or should I send the link to you?"

**Why it works:** "not selling anything" + "already built it" + an easy out (send the link). You are giving, not asking.

**When you reach the dentist:**

> "Dr. [Name] — quick one. I build websites for dental practices, and I rebuilt yours over the weekend as a sample. It's live right now, you can look at it on your phone. Three things I changed: [specific thing 1], [specific thing 2], and booking now takes one tap instead of a contact form.
>
> Can I text you the link? Take a look, and if you hate it, delete it — costs you nothing."

Then: **shut up.** Let them talk first.

**Objection — "how much?"**
> "£449 for the full build, live in seven days. But look at it first — I don't want you paying for something you haven't seen."

**Objection — "we already have a website"**
> "I know, I've been on it — that's how I rebuilt it. The issue isn't that it exists, it's that on mobile a patient has to scroll past four screens before they find how to book. Most practices lose that patient. Let me send the link, thirty seconds of your time."

**Objection — "send me an email"**
> "Will do. What's the best address? And can I call you Thursday afternoon to get your thoughts — or is Friday better?"
> *(Always leave with a scheduled next step.)*

### 6.2 Instagram DM — 3 touches

**DM 1**
> Hi [Name] — I build websites for dental practices. I rebuilt yours as a sample over the weekend, it's live. Want the link? No cost, just want your opinion on it.

**DM 2 (48 hrs later, only if no reply)**
> Following up — here it is anyway: [link]. Have a look on your phone, the booking flow is the main change. Let me know what you think 👍

**DM 3 (4 days later)**
> No worries if it's not the right time. If you ever want it, it's yours — I built it for you either way.

Cap at **25 DMs/day per account** or Instagram will limit you.

### 6.3 Email — 5 touches

Your current copy is good. The problem was never the copy — it was that follow-ups never sent and everything landed in spam. Keep touch 1 and 2, add three more:

| # | Day | Subject | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | `your site` | Existing copy — "I built you a version" |
| 2 | +3 | `Re: your site` | Existing follow-up |
| 3 | +7 | `[Practice] — booking flow` | **Link the live demo directly.** One specific problem you fixed. |
| 4 | +12 | `one thing I noticed` | Pure value, no ask — one mobile issue on their current site |
| 5 | +18 | `closing your file` | Breakup email. Consistently the highest-reply message in any sequence. |

**Breakup email (touch 5):**
> Hi [Name] — I'm going to assume the timing isn't right and close your file so I stop cluttering your inbox.
>
> The site I built is here if you ever want it: [link]. It'll stay live for another two weeks.
>
> All the best with the practice.
> Milin

### 6.4 The sales call — 6 steps

1. **Frame (30s)** — "I've got fifteen minutes booked. I want to show you what I built and hear what you'd change. If it's not for you, that's completely fine."
2. **Diagnose (3 min)** — *Ask, do not pitch.* "How do most new patients find you right now?" · "What happens when someone lands on your site — where do they go?" · "How many new patients a month would actually make a difference?"
3. **Screen-share the demo (5 min)** — Walk their *own* rebuilt site. Point at three specific changes and tie each to bookings.
4. **Price (30s)** — "£449 for the full build, live in seven days. £97 a month after if you want me handling hosting and edits — completely optional."
5. **Silence.** Say the price and stop talking. Whoever speaks first loses.
6. **Close** — "Want me to start on it? I take half up front, half when it's live."

**"I need to think about it"** →
> "Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think about — the price, or whether the site's right?"

That question surfaces the real objection. Handle *that*.

## 7. Getting paid

- **50% deposit up front, 50% on go-live.** Never build in full on a promise.

- **Cap revisions at the tier limit in writing.** "1 revision round" means one, and it must say so.
- Deliver in **7 days**, always. Fast delivery is your entire competitive advantage over agencies.
