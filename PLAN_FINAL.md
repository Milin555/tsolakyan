## The Loom video — do this before anything else

This is your replacement for a sales call. Everything depends on it.

**For each prospect, record 3 minutes:**

1. **(0:00–0:20)** Your face on camera. *"Hi Dr. [Name], Milin here — 3 minutes, I built you something."*
2. **(0:20–1:00)** Their **current** site or Instagram on screen. Point at one real problem. *"On mobile, a patient scrolls past four screens before they find how to book."*
3. **(1:00–2:30)** Screen-share **their rebuilt site.** Click through it. Show booking working in one tap.
4. **(2:30–3:00)** *"It's live at this link. It's yours to look at either way. If you want it as your real site, reply and I'll tell you how it works."*

**Why this works:** they see your face, hear your voice, and watch their own practice improved. That is 80% of what a sales call does — without you ever picking up a phone.

**Loom shows you when they watched it.** Someone who watched 90% is a hot lead. Follow up that same day.

Batch-record 10 Looms in one sitting. Roughly 45 minutes.

### Account safety — the #1 way this fails

**A new Instagram account cold-DMing gets banned within a day.**

- **Aged account only** — 6+ months, real photo, 15+ posts, real followers
- **Never automation.** Every DM by hand. Tools get you banned permanently.
- **Ramp:** 5–10/day week 1 → 15/day week 2 → 25/day cap. Never above 25.
- **"Action Blocked" → stop for 48 hours.** Pushing through kills the account.

If your account is under 6 months old, start warming it today and lean on contact forms and Upwork for week 1.

### The warm-up pass — triples your reply rate

Cold DMs from a non-follower land in **Message Requests**, which most people never open.

- **Day 1:** follow them, like 2–3 posts, view their story
- **Day 2:** send the DM

Reply rate goes from ~5% to **15–20%**. Twenty extra minutes a day. Non-negotiable.

### The DM sequence

**DM 1** *(after engaging yesterday)*
> Hi [Name] — saw your [specific real thing: veneer case, clinic tour, before/after]. Genuinely impressive work.
>
> Noticed you don't have a website yet. I build them for dental practices — I put together a version for you this weekend, it's live now.
>
> Want the link? Free, I just want your honest opinion.

**DM 2** *(48 hrs, no reply)* — **this is where the Loom goes**
> Here it is anyway: [live link]
>
> Made you a quick 3-min walkthrough too: [Loom link]
>
> Main thing — booking is one tap on mobile. Have a look 👍

**DM 3** *(4 days later)*
> All good if the timing's off. It's yours either way, I built it for you.

Stop after three. Never a fourth.

### When they reply positively — closing over text

> "Glad you like it! It's yours — $497 for the full build, live in 7 days.
>
> Works like this: half now, half when it's live. I'll send a payment link, then I need your logo, photos, and opening hours. That's it from your side.
>
> Want me to start?"

**"Too expensive"** →
> "No problem — I do a 3-page version at $297 that covers the essentials. Want me to show you what that looks like?"

*Never drop below $297. Downsell the scope, not the price.*

**"Let me think"** →
> "Of course. Can I check back Thursday? And what's the part you want to think about — the price, or whether the site's right for you?"

That question surfaces the real objection.
