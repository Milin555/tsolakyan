About this job directory

FrogHire keeps this jobs hub source-oriented and focused on active original apply pages rather than stale job detail archives.

Where do FrogHire.ai jobs come from?

FrogHire.ai organizes recent job postings from original company career pages and ATS sources, including Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, and more.

Are these jobs copied from LinkedIn or Indeed?

This directory is built around original company and ATS apply pages rather than reposted job-board listings. Apply links point back to the source page when available.

What is an ATS job source?

An ATS job source is the hiring system a company uses to publish and manage openings. Common ATS platforms include Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and SmartRecruiters.

How fresh are the public jobs on FrogHire.ai?

The public jobs directory is designed for recent active openings. FrogHire.ai account recommendations may update faster and can rank jobs against your resume, search goals, and saved preferences.

Do I apply through FrogHire.ai?

No. Public job cards send you to the original company or ATS apply page when an application link is available.

Why does FrogHire.ai use role pages instead of job detail pages?

Individual job postings expire quickly, so FrogHire.ai keeps stable role pages like software engineering jobs or data analyst jobs instead of building stale public job-detail archives.

How are jobs grouped by role?

Jobs are grouped by searchable role labels and broader career areas, while the underlying data keeps standardized function names for consistent filtering.

Is this jobs directory only for international students?

No. The public jobs directory is useful for any job seeker who wants source-oriented company career page and ATS listings. FrogHire.ai account features add more personalized search tools.
